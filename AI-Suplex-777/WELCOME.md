> [!tip] **🧠 Boot Sequence — Context Kick-start**
> The `AI-Suplex Kick-start/Context Kick-start/` folder is read during the AI agent boot sequence.  
> Place files here that your AI should know at startup:
> - **Profile.md** — Who you are, your role, skills, goals
> - **Active/** — Current session summary, recent wins, blockers
> - **Archived/** — Past context (moved here when no longer relevant)
>
> Every time an agent reads `AGENTS.md`, it loads this folder for live context.  
> Keep Profile.md stable. Update Active/ after each major session.
